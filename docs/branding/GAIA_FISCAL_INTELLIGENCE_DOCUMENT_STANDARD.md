# GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE — Document Standard

This standard applies to customer-facing and demonstration PDF/Excel deliverables generated by Gaia Fiscal Intelligence.

## Brand name

Use **GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE** as the document brand.

Do not place `gaialabai.com`, GaiaLab, or biology-product branding in Gaia Fiscal Intelligence deliverables.

## Paid deliverables

- Header/title: `GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE`
- Subtitle: `Governed Public-Finance Intelligence`
- Watermark: `GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE`
- Footer: order ID, jurisdiction/scope where available, generation date, `GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE`
- Excel workbook metadata and sheet branding must use `GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE`.

## Demonstration/sample deliverables

- Header/title: `GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE`
- Subtitle: `Governed Public-Finance Intelligence`
- Watermark: `SAMPLE — GAIA FISCAL INTELLIGENCE — NOT FOR RESALE`
- Prominent notice: `SAMPLE / DEMONSTRATION ONLY / NOT FOR RELIANCE IN FORMAL DECISION-MAKING`
- Demo files must never be represented as paid production orders.

## Evidence integrity

Branding and watermarking must not alter the governed artifact. Source organization, source URL, verification status, evidence capture time, and SHA-256 fingerprints remain part of the evidence record where available.
